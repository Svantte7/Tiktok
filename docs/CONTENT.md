## Jasmin Obscure

**EN tagline:** For those for whom fragrance is part of their own identity — not the expectations of others.

**EN body:** Jasmin Obscure is a darkly elegant atelier fragrance where northern lilac meets southern jasmine. In the opening, rose petals and lilac blossom bring a cool beauty and refined softness to the scent. Its true character is revealed on skin: in the heart, jasmine deepens into something intimate, shadowed and almost hypnotic. Oud, sandalwood, amber, benzoin and myrrh give the fragrance a warm, resinous and mysterious base. Jasmin Obscure is not a light floral fragrance. It is a presence — quiet, yet unforgettable.

**FI tagline:** Hänelle, jolle tuoksu on osa omaa identiteettiä — ei muiden odotuksia.

**FI body:** Jasmin Obscure on tumman elegantti atelier-tuoksu, jossa pohjoisen syreeni kohtaa etelän jasmiinin. Avauksessa ruusun terälehdet ja syreeninkukka tuovat tuoksuun viileää kauneutta ja hienostunutta pehmeyttä. Sen todellinen luonne paljastuu vasta iholla: sydämessä jasmiini syvenee intiimiksi, varjoisaksi ja lähes hypnoottiseksi. Oud, santelipuu, ambra, bentsoiini ja mirha antavat tuoksulle lämpimän, hartsisen ja salaperäisen pohjan. Jasmin Obscure ei ole kevyt kukkaistuoksu. Se on läsnäolo — hiljainen mutta unohtumaton.

**Notes / Nuotit:**
- Top notes: Lilac Blossom, Rose Petals
- Heart notes: Jasmine Sambac, Jasmine Grandiflorum
- Base notes: Oud, Sandalwood, Amber, Benzoin, Myrrh
- Nuances: Vanilla Resin, Powdery Warmth, Licorice, Soft Smoke

## Oud Nocturne

**EN tagline:** For the man whose elegance and strength are not an act, but part of his being.

**EN body:** Oud Nocturne is a dark and masculine atelier fragrance, where oud, black cherry and Madagascar vanilla form the strong backbone of the scent. In the opening, Pink Pepper brings a dry spark, while Black Cherry and Cherry Almond add a dark, almost velvety fruitiness to the composition. At the heart, the cherry deepens, ambered woods begin to rise, and the airy radiance of Hedione gives the fragrance room to breathe. In the base, oud, sandalwood, benzoin, myrrh and Madagascar vanilla merge into a warm, resinous and softly smoky structure. The vanilla does not make the scent sweet; instead, it gives it exotic depth and quiet softness. Oud Nocturne is masculine, but not harsh. Elegant, but not ornamental. It is the scent of dark wood, warm resin and confident silence — for a man whose presence carries calm, strength and moral steadiness.

**FI tagline:** Hänelle, jonka eleganssi ja voima eivät ole esitystä, vaan osa olemusta.

**FI body:** Oud Nocturne on tumma ja maskuliininen atelier-tuoksu, jossa oud, musta kirsikka ja Madagaskarin vanilja rakentavat tuoksun vahvan selkärangan. Avauksessa Pink Pepper tuo kuivaa säväystä, kun taas Black Cherry ja Cherry Almond antavat kokonaisuuteen tumman, lähes samettisen hedelmäisyyden. Sydämessä kirsikka syvenee, ambraiset puut nousevat esiin ja Hedionen ilmavuus antaa tuoksulle tilaa hengittää. Pohjassa oud, santelipuu, bentsoiini, mirha ja Madagaskarin vanilja sulautuvat lämpimäksi, hartsiseksi ja pehmeän savuiseksi rakenteeksi. Vanilja ei tee tuoksusta makeaa, vaan antaa sille eksoottista syvyyttä ja hiljaista pehmeyttä. Oud Nocturne on maskuliininen, mutta ei kova. Elegantti, mutta ei koristeellinen. Se on tumman puun, lämpimän hartsin ja itsevarman hiljaisuuden tuoksu — miehelle, jonka läsnäolossa on rauhaa, voimaa ja selkärankaa.

**Notes / Nuotit:**
- Top notes: Pink Pepper, Black Cherry, Cherry Almond
- Heart notes: Dark Cherry, Hedione Radiance, Amber Woods
- Base notes: Oud, Sandalwood, Madagascar Vanilla, Benzoin, Myrrh
- Nuances: Resinous Warmth, Powdery Vanilla, Licorice, Soft Smoke

## Velvet Carnival

**EN tagline:** For the one who carries the light of summer and the mystery of night.

**EN body:** Velvet Carnival is a luminous, velvety gourmand fragrance where the sweetness of grape, cotton-candy sugar and soft vanilla unfold like a summer evening carnival just before dusk. It is playful, but not childish — sweet, yet more grown-up in tone, softly seductive and warm. On the skin, the fragrance deepens into musk, amber and vanilla warmth. Velvet Carnival is a small fantasy: bright and delicious, yet softly mysterious beneath the surface — like the glow of a carnival lingering in the air after the music has faded.

**FI tagline:** Hänelle, joka kantaa kesän valoa ja yön salaperäisyyttä.

**FI body:** Velvet Carnival on valoisa ja samettinen gourmand-tuoksu, jossa rypäleen makeus, hattaramainen sokeri ja pehmeä vanilja avautuvat kuin kesäillan karnevaali juuri ennen hämärää. Se on leikkisä, mutta ei lapsellinen — makea, mutta sävyltään aikuisempi ja pehmeän viettelevä. Iholla tuoksu syvenee myskiksi, amberiksi ja vaniljaiseksi lämmöksi. Velvet Carnival on pieni fantasia: vaalea ja herkullinen, mutta taustaltaan pehmeän mystinen — kuin karnevaalin valo, joka jää ilmaan vielä musiikin hiljennyttyä.

**Notes / Nuotit:**
- Top notes: Grape, Cotton Candy, Summer Air
- Heart notes: Vanilla, Soft Musk, Gourmand Sugar
- Base notes: Amber Woods, Ambroxan, Velvet Musk
- Nuances: Powdery Sweetness, Caramelized Sugar, Soft Mystery

Every composition is developed from the first idea to the final accord, guided by intuition, craftsmanship, and patience. Our perfumes are crafted in small batches, shaped through experimentation and careful refinement rather than market trends. Timeless elegance that does not follow time, but endures beyond it. Inspired by the quiet sophistication of Europe's old towns, our creations unite modern perfumery with enduring sensibility — contemporary in expression, yet rooted in permanence. Old Town Atelier Parfums creates fragrances for those who value individuality over imitation, and character over fashion. Original compositions. Crafted with intention. Worn with confidence. Porvoo — Finland

## Founder's Story (vain EN saatavilla)

Heading: **Old Town Atelier Parfums — Founder's Story**

I have always believed that fragrance is one of the purest forms of respect — a silent gesture of consideration toward others, and at the same time a deeply personal art. Scents have followed me throughout life as invisible signatures of moments, places, and people.

Despite a lifelong appreciation for perfume, I never found the fragrance I truly wanted to call my own. Something was always missing — too familiar, too predictable, too close to something already known. Many perfumes were beautiful, even exceptional, yet rarely unforgettable. I kept searching for the kind of scent that makes you instinctively turn your head — the fleeting moment when time seems to pause as someone passes by, carrying quiet confidence and effortless elegance.

Eventually, I understood that if I could not find it, I would have to create it.

The idea grew slowly. Creation began long before the first formula was written — through observation, curiosity, and a desire to craft something original, something with soul.

Porvoo's Old Town became the natural birthplace of this vision. Its cobblestone streets, unhurried rhythm, and timeless atmosphere embody the qualities I believe perfume should possess: character, depth, and permanence. A place where nothing feels rushed and everything carries a story.

Old Town Atelier Parfums was born from this spirit.

I create fragrances in small batches, guided by intuition and experimentation rather than trends. Each composition is built from the ground up — not to replicate or reinterpret what already exists, but to become something entirely its own.

I want to compose perfumes that express strength without noise and elegance without effort — fragrances with presence, the kind that subtly transform the air around a self-assured person.

For myself, I continue creating the fragrance I once searched for: personal, distinctive, and authentic.

This house exists for those who value individuality over imitation, and artistry over convention — for those who believe perfume is not merely worn but expressed.

Old Town Atelier Parfums. Created with conviction. Crafted with intention. Born in Porvoo, Finland.
